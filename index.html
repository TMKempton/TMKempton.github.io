About

Current position and background

I am a senior lecturer in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Manchester, working in the dynamical systems group.

My mathematical research has focused on thermodynamic formalism, fractal geometry, Bernoulli convolutions, and self-affine sets. My research has been partially funded by EPSRC grant EP/T010835/1.

I previously held positions at St Andrews and Utrecht, and completed my PhD at Warwick in March 2011 under the supervision of Mark Pollicott. My thesis is available here.
