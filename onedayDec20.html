One Day Ergodic Theory Meeting

Zoom meeting held on Wednesday 2 December 2020.

This meeting was part of a collaborative series between the universities of Bristol, Exeter, Loughborough, Manchester, Queen Mary, St Andrews and Warwick, supported by a Scheme 3 grant from the London Mathematical Society.

Programme

2:00pm to 3:00pm

Dan Thompson (Ohio State)

Stronger ergodic properties for equilibrium states in non-positive curvature.

Abstract. Equilibrium states for geodesic flows over compact rank 1 non-positive curvature manifolds and sufficiently regular potential functions were studied by Burns, Climenhaga, Fisher and Thompson. The work showed that if the higher rank set does not carry full topological pressure then the equilibrium state is unique. This talk described recent results on the dynamical properties of these unique equilibrium states, including the Kolmogorov property and asymptotic central limit behaviour for approximations by regular closed geodesics.

3:30pm to 4:30pm

Reem Yassawi (Open University)
